**Proposal (OC-WEB-2025-001 §8) — Parliamentary & Ministerial Accountability Tracker:**

This split keeps Phase 2 from taking the whole Queen Mother module (Phase 2 is already the heavier GHS 40,650 block) and uses remaining Phase 3 capacity (GHS 28,500 still due).
